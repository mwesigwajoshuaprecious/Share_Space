PROJECT DESCRIPTION:


ShareSpace Hostel Borrowing Club

Introduction:


ShareSpace is a simple application designed to help university students borrow useful items from one another. A student may need something such as a calculator, textbook, cooking utensil, or extension cable for a short time. Another student may own that item but not be using it.

The application lets students list items they are willing to lend, browse items listed by other students, and send borrowing requests to the item owners. Owners can approve or reject requests, and borrowers can record when they return an item.

Problem statement:


Students sometimes need items that they do not own, while other students nearby may have those items unused. Without a simple way to find and request them, students may spend money buying items they need only temporarily or have difficulty finding someone willing to lend them one.
